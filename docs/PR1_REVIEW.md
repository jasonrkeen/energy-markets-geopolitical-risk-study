# PR #1 methodological and implementation review

Reviewed October 26, 2026 against initial draft commit `9a33580c9b3314dff98b1c8bdbb217fa4851d98e`. This is a source/code review with independent calculation checks, not an independent external reviewer endorsement. The PR remains a draft; main, original notebook, original retained inputs and V1 outputs are unchanged. The initial delivery bundle is preserved separately.

**Decision:** the repaired workflow is review-ready for explicitly qualified, retrospective exploratory associations. It is not a validated causal, predictive or investment-return model. Missing original-vintage evidence prevents exact historical reproduction; it does not by itself invalidate a clearly identified current-vintage exploratory estimate.

## Results and validation

- The crisis-window GPR coefficient remains 0.007189 per 100 GPR index points. Its calendar-HAC(6) interval is **[-0.000262, 0.014640]**, p=**0.05855**; HC3 gives p=**0.15701**. Neither result justifies a stable effect claim. The supplementary calendar-HAC result is outside the declared eleven-model Holm family and is exploratory; no expanded familywide significance claim is made.
- V1 replay still executes all 33 cells and closely matches the saved 308-observation regression. Exact historical provenance remains unresolved. Rich notebook tail-expression rendering and exchange/issuer source reconstruction are not certified.
- **33 tests pass**, including new failure-path, source-integrity, calendar-HAC and synthetic daily-data-to-model checks. The pinned clean environment passes `pip check`. The workflow was rerun twice offline and all **11 numerical CSVs match** after CRLF-to-LF normalization. The manifest binds 18 top-level output artifacts, excluding itself. CI results are checked at the final pushed revision rather than inferred from earlier runs.
- The previous coefficient plot is unchanged; its underlying coefficients and confidence limits were verified against the initial draft. No new visual claims are introduced.

## Remaining gates for a defensible Version 2

1. **Resolve the estimand and independently validate the proxies before strengthening substantive claims.** CL=F is an undocumented stitched futures quote, not a collateralized futures strategy return. Dollar differences and removal of the crisis window do not establish roll mechanics. Choose spot-price exposure or an investable strategy, obtain a documented source and reconcile the chosen series. XLE adjusted closes and the S&P price index also require explicit treatment if the claim becomes a total-return/factor-model claim.
2. **Resolve GPR measurement and source lineage.** A calendar-day mean of daily GPR is not established as equivalent to the publisher's monthly series. Compare definitions and values using separately recorded vintages. Historical download receipts and original daily market inputs are still missing; only a partial current-vintage replay is defensible. The frozen Git fingerprints establish preservation, not source authenticity.
3. **Assess stability and dynamic misspecification before promoting exploratory coefficients.** Residual Ljung–Box p≈0.017 remains a warning. HAC changes uncertainty estimates; it does not cure omitted dynamics, simultaneous market/oil/GPR shocks, structural breaks, nonstationarity or correlation between lagged dependent variables and errors. No stationarity, parameter-stability or alternative-shock identification program was completed here. A larger battery chosen after seeing results would itself require careful multiplicity and sensitivity reporting.
4. **Causal or predictive use requires a different validation stage.** Contemporaneous regressions and calendar lags do not identify mediation. None of these models has a real-time release-vintage information set or untouched out-of-sample performance assessment. Freeze hypotheses, timing and benchmarks before collecting an unexamined future evaluation period. Do not relabel the inspected historical sample a holdout.
5. **Finish calendar/provider reconciliation.** The four-day endpoint screen catches long stale periods, not every missing trading session, early close or timestamp mismatch. The three known cross-market date differences remain documented and have a small observed sensitivity, but an exchange-calendar audit is still incomplete. The staged publisher assumes one writer and is not a concurrent-run or sudden-power-loss transaction service; retained backups support recovery.

The next useful research milestone is a declared oil estimand plus a separately dated GPR monthly/market-source reconciliation. Passing tests, preserved files and corrected standard errors do not substitute for those decisions and evidence.

Method reference: statsmodels documents that its standard [HAC covariance assumes consecutive, equally spaced observations](https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/generated/statsmodels.stats.sandwich_covariance.cov_hac.html). The added calendar-HAC calculation and assumptions are specified in [METHODOLOGY_V2.md](METHODOLOGY_V2.md), with all original source references retained there.
